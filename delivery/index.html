Before delivery day6 things to finish in the days before you pick up
  1. Finish your purchase steps in the Rivian app. Upload proof of insurance, sign contracts, and submit final payment before delivery. Insurance rules vary by state — check Rivian's state list.
  2. Trading in a vehicle? Rivian asks you to bring the physical title on delivery day if you hold it.
  3. Ordered without a referral code? Ask your Rivian Guide to add one before delivery is complete — how it works.
  4. Set up the Rivian app and phone key so you can test it at pickup instead of setting it up there.
  5. Run the NHTSA recall check on your VIN the night before at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
  6. Plan for the inspection. Allow about 45 minutes, inspect in good light, and bring a charged phone for photos and its flashlight for the undercarriage. Pick your model below so only the checks that apply are shown.

Exterior
Full walk-around in good light
0/15
Vehicle matches your order confirmation
Cross-check exterior color, interior color/material, wheel spec, battery pack, and all packages against the window sticker.
Critical
Panel gaps consistent — doors, fenders, hood
Check door, fender and hood gaps from several angles. Document any that look uneven before signing.
Important
Paint — no chips, scratches, or swirl marks
Check all four corners and the hood leading edge in daylight or under the service center lights.
Important
Bumpers and body panels match the body color
Look at the front and rear bumpers next to the fenders in daylight. Bumpers are painted separately from the body, so a shade mismatch is worth checking. Don't accept a "we'll swap it later" promise without it in writing.
Important
All doors open, close, and latch smoothly
Test every door and exterior handle plus the frunk, tailgate (R1T), or liftgate (R1S / R2). Check that the weather seals are seated and undamaged. A door that won't latch is worth escalating before signing.
Critical
Front turn signals — both sides flash correctly
Recall 26V-508 (Rivian FSAM-1833), an August 2026 expansion of the original 25V-387 campaign, covers 2025–2026 R1T/R1S whose front turn signals may fail to illuminate. Test left and right through a full blink cycle. Safety issue — don't skip.
Critical
Headlights — no condensation, all beam modes work
Check for moisture inside the housing. Test DRL, low beam, and high beam, and watch for a "Low beam lights not working" message.
Critical
Rear lights — taillights, brake lights, rear turn signals, hazards
Walk around with the lights on, then turn on the hazards and confirm all four corners flash. Have someone press the brake while you check every brake light, including the center one.
Critical
Wheels — correct spec, no curb rash
Verify size and color match your order. Check all four for transport damage, and confirm every lug nut and valve-stem cap is in place.
Important
Tires — no sidewall damage, reasonable pressure
Check sidewalls for bulging or cuts. Glance at PSI on all four corners via the instrument cluster.
Important
Undercarriage — no shipping straps or transport blocks left on
Crouch and look under all four corners for transport straps, blocks, loose or hanging panels, and any signs of fluid leaks.
Critical
Reinforced underbody shield present (if on sticker)
Standard on Quad and included with the All-Terrain Package. If it's on your window sticker, look underneath and confirm it's actually installed.
Important
Frunk — opens, closes, seal intact, sub-tray present
Check that the rubber perimeter seal sits evenly with no gaps, and that the under-tray is present.
Important
Charge port opens, closes, and accepts a cable
Test manually and via the Rivian app. 2026+ Rivian vehicles have a native NACS port and use a Combo CCS1 DC Adapter at CCS fast chargers; 2022–2025 R1s have a CCS1 port and use a NACS DC adapter at Tesla Superchargers.
Critical
Powered tonneau cover operates smoothly R1T
Included on current R1T configurations. Some R1 Shop inventory is listed without a tonneau, so check your window sticker. Test open, close, and stop, and check the seal along all four edges.
Important
Gear Tunnel doors flush and latch easily R1T
Open and close both sides. Each door should latch without excessive force and sit flush with the body.
Important
Tow hitch and tow package present (if ordered)
If towing equipment is on your order or window sticker, confirm the hitch receiver is installed and the trailer wiring connector is present.
Important
Glass & Wipers
Windshield, windows, pano roof, wipers
0/5
Windshield — no chips, cracks, or edge delamination
Inspect at an angle to catch chips and star cracks. Check edges for white haze or bubbling at the perimeter.
Critical
Side, rear, and pano roof glass — no chips or cracks
Look from outside at an angle in daylight to catch chips and hairline cracks. Check the liftgate glass on R1S and R2 carefully.
Important
Rear drop-down window lowers and raises fully R2
On R2 Performance and Premium, the liftgate glass drops fully into the liftgate. Run it all the way down and back up, and confirm it seals with no gap, rattle, or wind-noise path.
Important
Electrochromic roof transitions fully (if equipped)
Standard on Tri and Quad; available on Dual Large and Max as part of the Sound + Vision Package. Switch it fully between opaque and clear and verify there are no stuck zones or partial patches.
Important
Wipers — no streaking, auto mode activates with water
Run wipers with washer fluid. If your vehicle has rain-sensing wipers, verify auto mode triggers.
Note
Interior
Trim, seats, accessories, and HVAC
0/15
Trim panels — no gaps, defects, or sharp seams
Run your hand along all trim edges. A defect in the wood/composite trim is easy to catch this way. Turn on every interior and ambient light while you're at it.
Important
Seats — no tears, stains, or stitching defects on any row
Check every row, including the bolsters and seams.
Important
Powered seat adjustments work in every direction — both fronts
Test every axis on both front seats. Also test second-row fold/slide, and third-row fold on R1S (if equipped).
Important
All windows go fully up and down
Test each window from its own switch and from the driver's door, plus any one-touch or all-windows control. Watch for windows that stop short, reverse, or don't seal at the top.
Important
All seat belts retract, latch, and lock under load — all rows
Pull each belt hard to confirm it locks. Listen for a rattle near the rear C-pillars — the warning sign in a 2022–2026 R1 recall for loose second-row retractor bolts (26V-009 / FSAM-1795).
Critical
No unusual odors
Mold, chemical, or musty smells can indicate water intrusion during transport. Lift the cargo floor and check for moisture underneath. Easier to flag at delivery than diagnose later.
Important
Glovebox(es) open, latch, and stay closed
Open and close every glovebox and storage compartment. The R2 has dual gloveboxes — check both.
Important
Heat produces warm air quickly
Set climate to max heat. Warm air should arrive within a few minutes; a long delay is worth flagging before you sign.
Critical
A/C cools properly — listen for unusual fan noise at high speed
Run A/C on max and listen at full fan speed. Clicking, rattling or a loud roar beyond normal fan noise is worth flagging before leaving.
Important
Seat heating and ventilation work (as equipped)
Test every level on both front seats, plus any heated rear seats (as equipped).
Note
Rivian Torch present in driver's door — powers on
The rechargeable Torch comes standard in the driver's door on R1 and on R2 Performance and Premium. Press to eject it and confirm it powers on.
Important
Air Compressor Kit present with hose and adapters
If equipped (standard on Quad; on other R1 trims check your window sticker), confirm the air hose kit and adapters are in the vehicle.
Important
Charging equipment on your order is present
All 2026+ Rivian vehicles come with a J1772 AC adapter; Quad configurations also include a Combo CCS1 DC adapter. Confirm these — plus any charger or adapters you added to your order — are physically in the vehicle. Estimate your home charging cost →
Important
Included items present — floor mats and any kits on your order
Compare against your order and window sticker. Items like floor mats and kits are easy to leave behind at the service center in the delivery rush.
Note
Any ordered accessories accounted for
Camp speaker, crossbars, roof tent, bike rack — verify each is physically in the vehicle. Missing accessories are far harder to chase after you drive away.
Important
Spare tire kit present (if ordered)
The R1 All-Terrain Package includes a spare (full-size on R1T); a compact spare is an optional add-on on every R2 trim. If it's on your order, verify the spare, jack and tools are present.
Important
Tech & Software
Screens, keys, connectivity, and recall software
0/11
Screens work — center touchscreen, driver display and rear screen
Tap all corners and edges of the 15.6" center screen (same size on R1 and R2) and look for dead zones or backlight bleed. Confirm the driver display shows speed and range, and on R1 that the rear console screen responds.
Critical
Every camera view displays clearly on the center screen
Shift to reverse and confirm the rear view appears promptly, then cycle through every camera view, including the overhead view. Look for blank, distorted or obstructed feeds, and check each lens outside for cracks.
Critical
Haptic Halo steering wheel controls respond with feedback R2
Test the steering wheel controls for climate, media, autonomy, and drive modes. Each should respond and give a clear haptic click.
Important
No active warning lights or fault codes on delivery
Confirm no active suspension faults, drivetrain alerts, or battery errors on either screen. If any warning is showing, do not sign without a written explanation and open service ticket.
Critical
Rivian app paired — test remote lock/unlock and horn honk
Verify the vehicle appears in your app. The app is your primary key — essential to confirm before leaving.
Important
Phone key — proximity unlock and walk-away lock both work
Walk away and confirm it auto-locks. Then approach and confirm it unlocks before you reach the handle.
Important
Every KeyCard and key fob works
Test each KeyCard to unlock and start the vehicle. If a key fob came with your order (the R2 Launch Package includes one), test it too. Fobs are otherwise sold in the Rivian Gear Shop.
Important
No pending recall-related software updates
Confirm there's no pending update in the Rivian app. Several recalls are fixed over the air — e.g. the September 2026 rearview camera recall (26V-597) needs software 2026.23+ on R1 or 2026.31.40+ on R2 — so a pending update can mean an open recall.
Important
Paid upgrades and subscriptions show as active
If you bought Autonomy+, the Performance Upgrade, Connect+ or any other upgrade, confirm it appears as active in the vehicle or the Rivian app before you leave.
Important
Bluetooth audio connects and all speakers work without distortion
Pair your phone, play music at moderate volume, and check each zone. Test at higher volume if you have an upgraded audio package.
Note
All USB-C ports functional — bring a cable and test
Test the front console and rear ports with your own cable.
Note
Power outlets work — 120V and 12V
Plug a phone charger or small device into every 120V outlet and the 12V socket and confirm each one powers on. Check the cabin and the cargo area. 120V outlets may need to be turned on from the center display first.
Note
Gear Guard camera feeds visible in app with clean images
Verify every camera feed shows a clear image in the app's live view.
Note
Road Test
10–15 min drive before signing — local streets only
0/7
Steering wheel centered when wheels are pointed straight
Before you move, sit with the wheels straight and check that the spokes are level. An off-center wheel needs an alignment visit to fix — flag it now.
Important
Air suspension cycles through all height modes (if equipped)
Standard on every R1. Cycle through the ride heights from lowest to highest and confirm full travel each way, with no suspension warnings.
Important
Vehicle tracks straight — no pull to one side
On a flat, straight road, briefly relax your grip. A consistent pull suggests an alignment issue — easier to document before signing.
Important
Brakes — no grinding or pulsation; regen transitions smoothly
Apply firm progressive braking at low speed and listen. Test regen by releasing the accelerator — should engage smoothly without lurching. Verify one-pedal Hold mode works.
Critical
Suspension — no clunks over bumps
Drive over a speed bump at low speed and listen for clunks from any corner. A single loud clunk is worth flagging and documenting.
Important
Steering feel — no vibration through the wheel while moving
Persistent vibration at any speed typically indicates a wheel balance issue or flat spot from transport. Test on two different road surfaces before dismissing it.
Important
No drivetrain whine or unusual noise under acceleration
Accelerate firmly on a quiet street and listen. A persistent whine at steady cruise is not normal. Highway-speed testing is fine to do on your drive home — you don't need freeway access at the service center.
Important
Recalls & Docs
Do the NHTSA check the night before — takes 2 minutes
0/6
NHTSA VIN check completed — all recall campaigns closed
Do this at nhtsa.gov/recalls the night before and confirm every campaign on your VIN is closed — the live feed in the sidebar lists current campaigns by model. Note: newly filed recalls can take weeks to show up in NHTSA's VIN search, so if you're taking delivery of a recently built vehicle, also ask the delivery specialist to confirm in writing that no open campaigns apply to your VIN. Buying a used R1? Ask for proof that all recall work is complete, including the rear toe link bolt recall (FSAM-1794) on 2022–2025 R1s. Bring a screenshot.
Critical
Window sticker and VIN saved for your records
Photograph the window sticker and the VIN (driver door jamb or base of the windshield). You'll need both for insurance, warranty claims and future recall lookups.
Note
Any delivery issues photographed with timestamps
Do this before leaving. Timestamped photos document that an issue was present at delivery.
Important
Service request filed in Rivian app for any noted issues
Open a service ticket before you leave so every issue is on record from day one, and ask the delivery specialist to note issues in your delivery paperwork.
Important
✓ Inspection Complete
All items checked. File any open service requests before you drive away — then go enjoy your Rivian.
